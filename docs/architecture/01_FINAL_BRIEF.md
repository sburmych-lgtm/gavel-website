## Who it is for

People who want a coach rather than a downloadable programme: beginners, weight
loss, muscle gain, strength and endurance, rehabilitation training, posture
correction, stretching, boxing.

Explicit exclusion, which the site must state: people for whom gym loading is
medically contraindicated.

## What the site must do

Sell coaching. The biography exists to make the coaching credible, not the other
way round. A visitor should be able to answer, in order: *is this for me* → *how
does he work* → *does it work* → *how do we work together* → *what does it cost*
→ *who is he* → *what do I do now*.

## Fixed decisions

- H1 is exactly **«Сила викована водою»**. Line breaks are free; wording is not.
- Water/canoe defines the first impression. No gym-first hero, no splash gate,
  no orange reference-site intro, no mythology, no generic black/red gym opening.
- A draggable Before/After slider is mandatory, using one genuine pair.
- `IGOR GAVRILEYKO` everywhere — never a second romanisation.

Permitted as secondary copy only: «Сильніше тіло. Чіткий курс.»,
«Не на віру — на заміри.», «Візьму за техніку. Не зламаю.»

## Content policy

Client-supplied information is approved first-party content. It does not require
third-party sourcing. What does get resolved: contradictions between client
sources, transcription errors, regulated or high-risk claims, and anything the
client asks to verify.

Guarded specifically: `Майстер спорту України` must never become
`міжнародного класу`. A previous prototype made exactly that upgrade. The build
fails on it.

Never invented: client counts, testimonials, ratings, timelines, kilograms,
phone numbers, currency conversions, credentials.

Two dataset items are published with deliberate care:

- «Реабілітація та фізіотерапія» is published as **реабілітаційний тренінг** —
  training, not medical treatment.
- «Підбір спортивного харчування та фармакології» is published as **підбір
  спортивного харчування** only. Pharmacology is not advertised on a public page;
  it routes to consultation.

Both are scope decisions, not content edits — they keep the page inside what a
trainer can publicly offer.

## Reference

`https://fit-and-you.webflow.io/` — structural inspiration only. Take rhythm,
whitespace, media scale, hierarchy, interaction quality. Take nothing of its
orange identity, exact styling or layout. Its mobile preloader is a
demonstration of what not to do.
